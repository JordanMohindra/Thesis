RESULTS PACK - all measured numbers
Compression of radar range-FFT data on Kintex UltraScale+ (xcku5p-ffvb676-2-e) @ 100 MHz
Dataset: ColoRadar cascade, 50 frames.   J. Mohindra z5418455, 17 Sep 2026; hardware updated 24 Sep 2026

Four designs throughout:
  DRHE-1     DRHE as originally implemented  (predicts from ramp m-1)
  DRHE-n     DRHE, TDM-corrected             (predicts from ramp m-12)
  LPC-1,2    LPC as originally implemented   (lags 1 and 2)
  LPC-n,2n   LPC, TDM-corrected              (lags 12 and 24)

LPC's synthetic ratios are lower because the 32,768-bit coefficient table is

and the 4.00 vs 3.84 gap is exactly that overhead.

append coder cannot leave incompressible data unchanged - it expands it.

11.92 x 2^30 / 1e8 = 127.99 bits/cycle = exactly 128 = the full AXI bus width

the gap by 7.4%.

Compare theoretical with theoretical: 6.40 vs 12.80 = a factor of 2, all from II.

     replica, 23-cycle pipeline               17,060    5,217    48    20   1    23   132.2

Measured throughput, co-sim @100 MHz: DRHE-1 5.14, step 3 (II=1) 8.76, replica 11.54 Gbit/s

WHAT EACH DIFFERENCE FROM KIEM IS:
  BRAM 2x   : 32-bit float state words vs his 16-bit. Replica = his 10 tiles exactly.
  DSP 3x    : every DSP is a float operator; 6x (264) at II=1 without sharing.
  II 2 vs 1 : one extra state write per cycle (3 accesses, 2 ports). Step 3 -> II 1.
  Depth 58  : 26 cycles closed loop + float operator latency (open-loop float = 32).
  LUT/FF    : float operators = 69% of DRHE-1 LUTs, 54% of FFs; packer ~4,200 LUTs.
  Replica vs Kiem's own build (2.68x LUT, 1.83x FF): speed grade + literal packer +
  23-cycle depth explain ~45% of the FF gap and ~5% of the LUT gap. The rest is not
  a property of the design he describes (tool 2022.2, or undescribed logic).
