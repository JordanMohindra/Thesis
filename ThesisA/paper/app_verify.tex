\chapter{Verification Record}
\label{app:verify}

Before this report was finalised, every result it depends on was re-run from
the current sources on 24 September 2026, and the compression results were
also recomputed by an implementation written independently of both the MATLAB
and the HLS code. This appendix records what was re-run, how, and whether the
result agreed with the number reported.

\section{The independent implementation}

\texttt{verify\_indep/indep.py} is a from-scratch Python and NumPy
implementation of the whole compression chain, written from the descriptions in
Chapters~\ref{ch:algorithms} and~\ref{ch:method} without reusing any code:

\begin{enumerate}
\item read a raw ColoRadar frame and apply the TDM flattening, per-ramp DC
removal, the 16-bit shift, a symmetric Hann window, the FFT divided by $N$, the
first 128 bins, and the FX16 scaling with MATLAB's round-half-away-from-zero;
\item DRHE at lag 1 and lag $n_{\mathrm{Tx}}$, in double precision with NumPy's
trigonometric functions, with the $-32768$ clamp and closed loop;
\item LPC at lags 1,\,2 and $n_{\mathrm{Tx}}$,\,$2n_{\mathrm{Tx}}$, with
Yule--Walker coefficients quantised as the HLS quantises them
(\texttt{ap\_fixed<16,2>} and \texttt{ap\_fixed<16,1>}, truncation towards
$-\infty$) and the 16-bit coefficient table charged;
\item the S4 + APPEND + fixed Huffman bit count, with and without 256-bit
padding.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Corrections made as a result}

The re-run and the reading of the sources that went with it led to the
following corrections to earlier versions of this report and of the project's
own documents.

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Kiem's data.} Earlier documents said his hardware was tested on
synthetic data and his algorithms on AWGN. Every compression result he
publishes is from recordings (Section~\ref{sec:kiem_data}).
\item \textbf{Coder overhead.} The ``\SI{6.2}{\percent} above entropy'' was
the overhead on DRHE's lag-1 residuals; on the raw FX16 codes it is
\SI{12.0}{\percent}.
\item \textbf{Edge-case table.} The random-input case is allowed, and produces,
a 1-LSB error from the $-32768$ clamp.
\item \textbf{Detection chain.} It has no angle FFT, and its Doppler FFT runs
across transmitter-interleaved ramps (Section~\ref{sec:refchain}).
\item \textbf{Toolchain.} MATLAB R2026a, not R2024b.
\item \textbf{Mixed windows.} Percentages that compared five-frame and
50-frame figures are now computed on 50 frames throughout
(Table~\ref{tab:fifty}).
\item \textbf{LPC at lag 1.} Its \emph{residuals} are slightly better than no
prediction; it loses to no prediction only once its coefficient table is paid
for (Section~\ref{sec:lpcshrink}).
\item \textbf{Two citations.} The quantisation-study reference of the Thesis~A
report attached a different paper's authors to Deng and Huang's title
\cite{deng2024}; and the peak raw sample of 914 is over the first five frames
(1094 over all fifty).
\item \textbf{Repository.} The TDM-corrected HLS sources existed only in the
build clone on \texttt{D:}; they are now committed with the rest.
\end{itemize}
